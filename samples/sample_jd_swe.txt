Senior Software Engineer - Distributed Systems & Cloud Platforms

About the Role:
We are seeking a talented Senior Software Engineer to join our Core Infrastructure & Platform team. In this role, you will architect, build, and scale resilient microservices and cloud infrastructure powering our mission-critical enterprise platform.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, implement, and maintain high-throughput backend services and APIs using Python, Go, or Node.js.
- Architect scalable cloud solutions on AWS utilizing Docker, Kubernetes, and Terraform.
- Optimize database queries and caching layers across PostgreSQL and Redis for low-latency transaction processing.
- Build and maintain continuous integration and deployment pipelines (CI/CD) to ensure reliable releases.
- Collaborate with cross-functional engineering, product management, and security teams in an Agile environment.
- Champion engineering best practices, conduct thorough code reviews, and mentor junior and mid-level developers.

Requirements & Qualifications:
- 5+ years of software engineering experience building production distributed backend systems.
- Strong proficiency in modern languages such as Python, TypeScript, Go, or Java.
- Proven experience with container orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes) and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform).
- Hands-on expertise with relational and non-relational databases (PostgreSQL, Redis, MongoDB).
- Familiarity with event-driven architectures (Kafka or RabbitMQ) and microservices design patterns.
- Strong communication, problem-solving, and cross-functional leadership skills.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.
